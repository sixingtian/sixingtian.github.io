This paper examines how coupon expiration time affects consumer acceptance using observational scenario-survey data (12,007 responses across 490 demographic profiles) and causal machine learning. Treating the two-hour (versus one-day) expiration window as an observational exposure identified under selection-on-observables, cross-fitted IPW estimates an 18.3 percentage-point reduction in stated acceptance (cluster-robust SE: 1.4 pp). Heterogeneity discovery is separated from confirmation through profile-cluster sample splitting, with causal forests and T-learners on the exploration sample and held-out contrasts by urgency, social context, and distance. Simulations show effect-based targeting reduces acceptance losses by 29–42% relative to random allocation under a fixed short-expiration budget.
