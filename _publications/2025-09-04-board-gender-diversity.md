Using linear and nonlinear panel models on 840 UK firm-year observations (2008–2016) with board-size, year, and industry fixed effects, this paper identifies an inverted-U relationship between the share of female directors and Tobin's Q, with a threshold around 20–25%, moderated by firm size.
