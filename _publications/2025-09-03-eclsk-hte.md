This paper estimates heterogeneous treatment effects of early childhood interventions on fifth-grade math achievement using a machine learning-augmented causal analysis of the ECLS-K:2011 longitudinal dataset (n ≈ 18,000). Causal random forests (R grf) are trained on multiply imputed, sample-weighted data, identifying an approximately 0.15 SD improvement in fifth-grade math scores among high-risk children.
