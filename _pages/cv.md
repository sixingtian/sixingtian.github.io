## Research Experience

**Correspondence Audit Studies on Labor Market Discrimination** (May 2026 – Present)  
Research Assistant, supervised by Prof. Sally Sadoff (University of California, San Diego)  
- Processed participant-level data from 10,000+ respondents (44,000+ observations) across 8 name-perception studies using Python, constructing race and gender perception accuracy measures in an analysis-ready one-row-per-participant format  
- Constructed a structured 53-sheet database tracing the provenance of 600+ fictitious names across 22 correspondence audit studies, verifying each source against original paper appendices

**When Click-to-Cancel Creates Complexity: A Theory of Subscription Regulation** (Mar 2026 – Present)  
Independent Research, University of Nottingham Ningbo China  
- Developed a partial-equilibrium model in which firms jointly choose cancellation friction and retention-menu complexity; proved a "waterbed effect" whereby capping friction under California's AB 2863 raises firms' return to complexity  
- Derived the welfare condition under which click-to-cancel regulation benefits consumers, showing that only comprehensive regulation constraining both instruments eliminates the waterbed effect

**Causal Machine Learning and Counterfactual Inference in Consumer Behavior** (Sep 2025 – Apr 2026)  
Undergraduate Dissertation, supervised by Prof. Zhenjiang Lin (University of Nottingham Ningbo China)  
- Applied causal machine learning methods (causal random forests, TMLE, BART) in R (grf, tmle) and Python (scikit-learn) to estimate average and heterogeneous treatment effects of coupon and advertising interventions on consumer behavior  
- Analyzed e-commerce clickstream and purchase data with SQL and Python, using MICE for missing values; designed a personalized coupon allocation framework with dashboards (R Shiny) to support targeting decisions under budget constraints

**Estimating HTE of Early Childhood Interventions on Fifth-Grade Math Achievement** (Mar 2025 – Sep 2025)  
Research Assistant, supervised by Dr. Jiaxi Yang (Columbia University)  
- Extracted and merged longitudinal ECLS-K:2011 data (n ≈ 18,000) using Python (pandas) and R (tidyverse), applying sample weights and multiple imputation (MICE) to handle missing values  
- Trained causal random forests (R grf) to estimate heterogeneous treatment effects of early childhood interventions, identifying an approximately 0.15 SD improvement in fifth-grade math scores among high-risk children

**Board Gender Diversity and Firm Performance in the UK** (Nov 2024 – Jun 2025)  
Research Assistant, supervised by Prof. Abigail Barr (University of Nottingham)  
- Estimated linear and nonlinear panel models on 840 firm-year observations (2008–2016) using Stata, with board-size, year, and industry fixed effects; tested for heteroskedasticity (White test) and moderation via firm-size interactions  
- Identified an inverted-U relationship between female director share and Tobin's Q (threshold around 20–25%), moderated by firm size; findings published as a co-authored paper in *Entrepreneurship and Innovation*
