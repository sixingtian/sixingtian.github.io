## About

I am **Xingtian (James) Si**, a researcher in **causal inference and causal machine learning**, with a focus on **consumer behavior, targeting, and policy evaluation**.

My interests center on estimating **heterogeneous treatment effects** and designing decision rules for **who to treat / what to recommend**, using modern tools such as **Double/Debiased Machine Learning (DML)**, **TMLE**, **causal forests**, and related methods. I am especially interested in bridging econometrics and machine learning to build **interpretable, policy-relevant** evidence and scalable targeting systems.

## Research Interests

- Causal inference & program evaluation (selection, identification, robustness)
- Causal ML (DML / TMLE / causal forests; HTE / uplift modeling)
- Targeting and personalization in marketing (treatment assignment, incremental impact)
- Applied microeconomics and data-driven policy design

## Current Projects

- **Causal ML for targeting**: estimating individual- and group-level treatment effects and constructing decision rules for allocation.
- **Consumer behavior applications**: modeling behavioral responses to interventions and exploring heterogeneity across subpopulations.
- **Empirical workflows**: reproducible pipelines for data cleaning, estimation, validation, and reporting.

(Details and materials are available in **Publications / Portfolio / CV**.)

- **Machine learning**: tree-based models, regularization, cross-fitting, model selection, predictive evaluation
